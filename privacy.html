Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Who we are

VoDanhAI uploader is a personal tool used by the owner of the VoDanhAI YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel. It is run only on the owner's own computer.

What Google data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which allows uploading videos to the signed-in user's YouTube channel. The app uses it only to send video files, titles, descriptions and settings that the owner has chosen to the owner's own channel.

The app does not read, list, edit or delete existing videos, comments, playlists, subscribers, analytics or any other data from the YouTube channel or the Google account.

How data is stored

The sign-in token that Google returns is stored in a file on the owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server and is not sent to anyone else. The app has no website backend, no database and no analytics or advertising trackers.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent or transfer Google user data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising or for training machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

VoDanhAI uploader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored token by deleting the token file on their computer.

Children's content

The app itself collects no information from viewers or children. Videos uploaded through it are published on YouTube, which applies its own policies.
