VoDanhAI uploader

A small personal tool for publishing videos to the VoDanhAI YouTube channel.

What it does

VoDanhAI uploader is a desktop script used by the owner of the VoDanhAI YouTube channel to upload finished videos (short animated stories for children) to that same channel through the YouTube Data API.

When the owner signs in, the app asks for one permission only: to upload videos to the owner's own channel (youtube.upload). It cannot read, edit or delete anything else on the channel or in the Google account. New uploads are created as Private, and the owner reviews each video before it is made public.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner uses this app. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
